This report documents the end-to-end lifecycle management of the "devops-release"
Helm chart deployment on a local Kubernetes cluster (Minikube). It verifies:
  1. Parameterized release upgrades via CLI overrides (`--set`) and `--reuse-values`.

  4. Release history tracking, revision increments, and secret storage management.

Technical Explanation of Hook Lifecycle & Delete Policy:
The hook manifest (`templates/hooks/pre-upgrade-backup.yaml`) defines:
  annotations:
    "helm.sh/hook": pre-upgrade
    "helm.sh/hook-weight": "1"
    "helm.sh/hook-delete-policy": "before-hook-creation,hook-succeeded"

Because `hook-delete-policy` contains `hook-succeeded`, Helm automatically and cleanly
deletes the completed Job and its Pod immediately after successful validation.

Technical Details:
  - Helm 3 does not rely on an in-cluster daemon (like Tiller in Helm 2).
  - Every release revision is preserved as an immutable Kubernetes Secret in the target
    namespace named `sh.helm.release.v1.<release-name>.v<revision>`.
  - The secret contains a gzipped, base64-encoded JSON representation of the release
    manifest, metadata, and user-supplied values.
  - Revisions 1 through 3 are marked `superseded`, while revision 4 is actively `deployed`.
